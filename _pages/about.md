I'm **Mostafa Othman**, a robotics researcher with an **M.Sc. in Robotics and Computer Vision** (Innopolis University) and a **B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering** (Nile University). I work on **robot manipulation under uncertainty**: contact-rich assembly, teleoperation, haptic interfaces, and vision-guided control.

This site hosts my **publications**, **thesis and lab projects**, and an academic **CV**.

---

## Research focus

- Peg-in-hole and tight-clearance assembly
- Bilateral and shared teleoperation with force feedback
- Computer vision for detection and localisation in clutter
- Learning from demonstration and hybrid position/force control

See [Research](/research/) for a fuller statement and [Projects](/portfolio/) for implementations and demos.
