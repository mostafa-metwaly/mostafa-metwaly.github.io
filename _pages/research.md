## Research statement

My research training focused on **contact-rich manipulation** when geometric uncertainty exceeds what position control alone can guarantee. Peg-in-hole assembly with tight clearance is a canonical benchmark: it requires combining **perception** (where is the hole?), **control** (how hard to push?), and **human-in-the-loop** strategies when autonomy is brittle.

At Innopolis University I studied this on a **KUKA LBR iiwa** with ROS, a 3D Systems Touch haptic device, and VR-based operator interfaces. My thesis compared **four control modes**: full autonomy, bilateral teleoperation with force feedback, shared autonomy, and learning from demonstration — with user studies across scenarios. That line of work extended [**hybrid vision/force control**](/publication/2021-08-26-robotic-pick-and-assembly/) and deep-learning-based picking, published at IEEE NIR 2021.
